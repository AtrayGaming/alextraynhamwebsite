I make things in a few different formats: videos, scripts, guest experience concepts, and training tools. Each project below shows my part in the work, something you can actually look at, and the thinking behind it.

  1. 01YouTube · Published work

    AtrayGaming, my YouTube channel

    I ran AtrayGaming myself: ideas, edits, titles, thumbnails, publishing, and audience engagement. By October 2026, the channel had 300+ videos since 2014 and 189,129 lifetime views. Six piano-cover and Beat Saber uploads account for about 54% of those views. The case study examines that pattern without treating the totals as proof of why it happened.

    See: published videos, original thumbnails, October 2026 analytics, and proposed packaging tests. Read the case study
  2. 02Content proposal · Independent

    One Group, Different Speeds

    I wrote a Celestial Park video pitch for a group with different ride preferences. My key choice was to give both friends a ride they wanted, then a shared ending. The proposal includes a five-minute episode and two vertical edits. It has not been filmed or launched.

    See: opening dialogue, a six-beat storyboard, thumbnail studies, and a pilot measurement plan. Read the case study
  3. 03Research · UCF

    Guest experience research

    I combined a literature review with a survey for a UCF research paper about expectations shaped by theme park content. There were three respondents. The case study shows what they said, what the sample cannot establish, and how I used the question in a guest-planning writing sample.

    See: the original paper, research findings and limits, and a complete writing sample. Read the case study
  4. 04Academic sponsorship proposal · 2025

    Villains Media Night

    For a team event concept, I developed the individual sponsorship proposal: partner tiers, brand activations, benefits, and a delivery timeline. The work connects a creative event idea to what a partner would actually receive. It is an academic proposal, not a produced event.

    Partner tiers · Brand activations · Delivery timeline Read the proposal (PDF)
  5. 05Guest experience concept · College project

    Curse Tracker

    I wrote an executive-summary concept for an interactive experience across Dark Universe. Guests choose a path through five checkpoints before a finale with Maleva. The design considers repeat visits, checkpoint spacing, and guest flow. It was not built or piloted.

    Guest journey · Storytelling · Operations considerations Read the case study

Writing

Two samples you can read right here.

A script has to sound like people. A planning card has to give someone a usable next step. Here’s how I approached each.

Original script · Independent proposal

A reason to keep watching

Friend A“I came for the coaster.”

Friend B“I came with you. Those are different things.”

I opened with the disagreement between the friends because that is the problem the video needs to solve. The ride information comes after the viewer understands the people.

Read the full opening and storyboard

Original planning copy · Hypothetical sample

Before you pick your next stop

Start with one thing each person wants from the day. A ride? Time to explore? A shared meal?

The sample starts with a decision a guest can make. It leaves room for different preferences and avoids promising exact timings. I wrote it to explore the research question; it has not been audience tested.

Training tools

Building what other trainers teach from.

These projects grew out of my work training new team members. The format matters: a learner can match, turn over, or handle something instead of only listening to an explanation.

Radio-codes curriculum

I created a physical flashcard set and a quick-match activity for new team members. Both give trainers a way to practice the material with someone and talk through what they missed.

Physical training materials. Operational content is not reproduced here.

Product aids trainees can hold

I worked with Guest Services on a laminated stroller pass and with the Express team to source voided Attraction Assistance Pass samples, regular and gold. Trainees can handle and recognize the products they are learning about.

Training aids made or sourced with partner departments.
