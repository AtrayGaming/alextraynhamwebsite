About Alex

It started with
“I could make that.”

YouTube videos first. Then theme parks, training tools, and a growing interest in why people respond to what we make.

I started with what I liked.

Gaming, music, and the communities around them. My channel, AtrayGaming, goes back to 2014: Minecraft episodes, Beat Saber, piano covers, streams. I handled the ideas, editing, titles, thumbnails, and publishing myself.

It wasn’t a neat career plan. It was a reason to make something, put it out there, and figure out the next one. Looking back at the channel now, I’m interested in the difference between what I wanted to make and what people actually watched.

That story, with the actual videos

Then I saw the other side of the experience.

I’ve worked in food and beverage at Disney and Universal, helped commission Epic Universe attractions, and now work in attractions operations at Constellation Carousel and Curse of the Werewolf.

In a video, you can edit an explanation until it works. In attractions, someone is standing in front of you, trying to understand what to do next. That’s made me pay attention to the gap between what we think we explained and what someone actually heard.
